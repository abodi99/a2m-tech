## Insights / articles (do not publish empty)

Route: `/sv/insikter/` (EN: `/en/insights/`) currently lists planned topics only.

Planned briefs (publish only when fact-checked):

1. **Från tilldelning till förvaltning: så skapas en uppföljningsbar leverans**  
   Intent: informational. Outline five-stage model + handover artefacts. Link to delivery capability.

2. **Vad bör en kontinuitets- och överlämningsplan innehålla?**  
   Intent: checklist. No proprietary methodology claims.

3. **Vilket underlag behöver en upphandlande organisation från en digital leverantör?**  
   Intent: commercial/due diligence. Link to procuring-organizations fact sheet.

4. **Så arbetar beställare och leverantör med avvikelser utan att tappa tempo**  
   Intent: process. Align with quality-security practices.

5. **Checklista för tydlig ansvarsfördelning i digitala uppdrag**  
   Intent: roles/RACI-style without jargon overload.

6. **Så utvärderar ni en långsiktig leveranspartner**  
   Intent: evaluation criteria; avoid unverified case outcomes.

## Post-launch validation

- Google Search Console + Keyword Planner for Swedish queries after indexation.
- Validate Organization JSON-LD with Rich Results Test.
- Revisit titles/meta if CTR is weak; keep claims verification-gated.

## Editorial rules

- No invented references, certifications, or savings metrics.
- Prefer concrete delivery language over buzzwords.
- Each article needs unique H1, meta, canonical, hreflang, and BreadcrumbList only when breadcrumbs are visible.
